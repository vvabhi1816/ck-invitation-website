PLACE YOUR PHOTOS HERE
======================

Drop the real photographs into this folder using these exact names:

  bride.jpg    → the bride's portrait        (used as a portrait AND a background)
  groom.jpg    → the groom's portrait        (used as a portrait AND a background)
  couple.jpg   → a photo of the couple       (used as the hero + couple banner background)
  og-image.jpg → social share preview image  (1200 x 630, for WhatsApp/Facebook previews)

Gallery photos go in the  gallery/  subfolder:

  gallery/photo-1.jpg
  gallery/photo-2.jpg
  ... (add as many as you like)

Until you add a file, the site shows an elegant styled placeholder instead of a
broken image. Paths can be changed in  config/assets.js.
