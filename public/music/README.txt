PLACE YOUR BACKGROUND MUSIC HERE
================================

Add an appropriately licensed audio track named:

  wedding-melody.mp3

The floating music button (bottom-right of the site) will play / pause it.
Music NEVER autoplays before a user interaction. Until you add a file, the
music control simply hides itself — nothing breaks.

Change the path in  config/assets.js  (weddingAssets.music) if you prefer a
different filename.

Do not use copyrighted music without permission.
